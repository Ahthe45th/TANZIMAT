### Notes
- Testing
- Got it. We’ll keep everything inside Instagram until the buyer is ready to pay, then hand off. Here’s the cleanest way to build that end-to-end using only Meta’s native tools.
- 
- The Instagram-only funnel
- 
- A) Get them into IG DMs
- 
- 1. Campaign: Create Ads that click to Instagram Direct in Ads Manager (Engagement/Traffic/Sales objectives all support it). Destination = Instagram Direct. This opens a DM thread from the ad. 
- 
- 
- 2. Creative: Your 1-minute videos (4 per ad set) + Advantage+ placements with your saved audience to push down CPI while the system learns. (You can cap daily budgets per ad set to control volume.)
- 
- 
- 
- B) Automate the conversation inside Instagram
- 
- Everything here is available in Meta Business Suite → Inbox → Automations for Instagram.
- 
- 1. Entry menu (FAQ)
- 
- Turn on Frequently Asked Questions for Instagram chats. Add your first four “icebreaker” buttons that map to your flow:
- 
- “Start” (or “Am I a fit?”)
- 
- “How it works (60s)”
- 
- “Packages & Pricing”
- 
- “Ready to proceed”
- 
- 
- 
- When someone opens the chat, these appear automatically and send your preset replies without a human. 
- 
- Tip: Keep answers short and include your first video as a DM attachment or link to a Reel. Then end with “Tap ‘Continue’ below.” (Next step handles that.)
- 
- 2. Step-by-step sequencing with Automations
- 
- Use Inbox Automations rules to chain the steps:
- 
- Rule 1 (after FAQ → Start):
- Trigger: “Message contains Start/Continue/1”
- Action: Auto-reply with Video 1 + a prompt: “Reply READY to continue.” Optionally add a timed follow-up (e.g., after 10 minutes: “Still with me? Reply READY to see the next 60s video.”) 
- 
- Rule 2 (qualification):
- Trigger: “Message contains READY”
- Action: Send Video 2 + 1 qualifying question with answer choices the user can type (“A/B/C”). Include “Reply A, B, or C.”
- Add a second auto-rule for each answer (A/B/C) that replies with the tailored line plus “Type PRICING to see packages.”
- 
- Rule 3 (pricing):
- Trigger: “Message contains PRICING”
- Action: Auto-reply with a concise packages card (text + image) and a CTA: “Type PROCEED to continue.”
- 
- Rule 4 (purchase intent):
- Trigger: “Message contains PROCEED”
- Action: Auto-reply: “Great. I’ll send the payment link now.” Also apply a label (e.g., “Ready to Pay”) so your team sees it instantly in the Inbox and knows to step in only now. 
- 
- 
- > Why this works: FAQs supply the buttons for the first click. Then keyword-based Inbox Automations advance the user through each stage, send videos, and branch on answers — all natively, no APIs, no WhatsApp.
- 
- 
- 
- 3. Lead gen in messaging (optional, still Instagram)
- 
- If you prefer a form-first version inside DMs, use Lead generation in Instagram Direct. It lets you qualify and nurture leads in the conversation and then continue your steps with automations. 
- 
- C) Hand-off to payment (only after they’re ready)
- 
- When the user types PROCEED, your automation replies with the payment link (e.g., your checkout page, M-Pesa/Flutterwave/Paystack link).
- 
- Your agent is alerted via the Inbox label and can jump in only if needed (e.g., confirming receipt or upselling a higher package).
- 
- 
- D) Guardrails so volume doesn’t swamp you
- 
- Budget caps per ad set and scheduled delivery (run heavier during staffed hours).
- 
- Use a “Just exploring” FAQ path that educates without surfacing pricing yet, so low-intent users self-select into a slower nurture branch.
- 
- Add timed follow-ups in Inbox Automations to rescue drop-offs without a human (e.g., 30/120-minute nudges).
